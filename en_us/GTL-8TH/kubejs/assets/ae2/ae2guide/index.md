# What is Applied Energy 2?

Applied Energy 2 adds various components and mechanisms for logistics and storage. You can replace your original large room of boxes with a compact ME network, and this is just the beginning of other content. Applied energy should work with and automate other mods in the mod pack. With just one click, you can have the system automatically synthesize every intermediate product and final product in a complex synthesis chain, or maintain the number of items in your inventory, synthesize more as needed, or simply transport items throughout the base.
