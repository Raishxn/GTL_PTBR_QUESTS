Meteorites are the starting point for AE2. They can provide certain key materials: various types of [Certus Quartz Mother Rock](../items-blocks-machines/budding_certus.md), and the <ItemLink id="mysterious_cube" /> in the center.

[Getting Started](../getting-started.md) details what to do after finding the meteorite.

## Looking for meteorites

Meteorites often leave huge craters in the terrain, so you've probably found a few. If it has not been encountered yet, <ItemLink id="meteorite_compass" /> will point to the nearest mysterious block.
