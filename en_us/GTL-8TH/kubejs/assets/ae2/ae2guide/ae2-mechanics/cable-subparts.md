# Cable subassembly

<GameScene zoom="4" background="transparent">
  <ImportStructure src="../assets/assemblies/subparts_demonstration.snbt" />
  <IsometricCamera yaw="195" pitch="30" />
</GameScene>

Certain [devices](devices.md) and components in AE2 can be placed in the same slot as a cable. This goes a long way in designing compact facilities.
Sneaking and right-clicking while holding <ItemLink id="certus_quartz_wrench" /> or <ItemLink id="network_tool" /> destroys subcomponents (or cables) without affecting other things in the same tile.
