Dye balls can be used to dye [cables.md], wool, terracotta, glass, concrete and other dyeable blocks. They can also be used <ItemLink id="matter_cannon" /> to shoot out a small pool of dye.

## Recipe

Place 8 matter balls around the dye.

They behave the same as normal dye balls, except that when fired in <ItemLink id="matter_cannon" />, the dye pool they leave behind will glow. Might be suitable for use as a flare gun.

## Recipe
