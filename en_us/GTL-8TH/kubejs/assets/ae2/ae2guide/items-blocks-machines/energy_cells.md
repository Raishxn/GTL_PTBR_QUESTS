#Energy Components

<Row gap="20">

  <BlockImage id="dense_energy_cell" scale="8" p:fullness="4" />

  <BlockImage id="creative_energy_cell" scale="8" />

Energy components give the network greater [energy](../ae2-mechanics/energy.md) capacity. A certain amount of energy cache can reduce the impact of energy spikes caused by large amounts of input and output. A larger energy storage capacity allows the network to operate when the power supply is disconnected (such as a solar panel array at night), and can also handle the huge instantaneous energy consumption generated by [spatial storage](../ae2-mechanics/spatial-io.md).

The filler bars on the side of the component correspond to its energy level.

*0 when full level is less than 25%.
*1 when full between 25% and 50%.
* 2 when full between 50% and 75%.
*3 when the charge level is between 75% and 99%.
* 4 when full level exceeds 99%.

## Component type

* <ItemLink id="energy_cell" /> can store 200kAE, which can easily handle the energy spikes of ordinary networks; usually, one per network is enough.
* <ItemLink id="dense_energy_cell" /> can store 1.6MAE, which is suitable for operating the network without energy supply and handling huge instantaneous energy consumption of large [spatial storage](../ae2-mechanics/spatial-io.md).
* <ItemLink id="creative_energy_cell" /> is a creative mode item used for testing and provides none! limit! able! quantity!
