[Fluix](fluix_crystal.md) Tools are similar to iron tools, but their durability is 3 times higher, and their attack damage and digging speed are higher.

All Froix tools can be considered as built-in Fortune I/Looting I, which is very useful when you don't have an enchantment table.

Need to make <ItemLink id="fluix_upgrade_smithing_template" />.
