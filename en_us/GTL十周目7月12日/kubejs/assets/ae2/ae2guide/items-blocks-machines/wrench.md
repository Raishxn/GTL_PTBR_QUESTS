# spanner

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="certus_quartz_wrench" scale="4" />

  <ItemImage id="nether_quartz_wrench" scale="4" />
</Row>

The wrench can be used to rotate AE2 equipment (right click) and remove AE2 blocks (Shift right click). Individual [subparts](../ae2-mechanics/cable-subparts.md) can also be removed without affecting other things on the same cable. (Cables can also be removed without disturbing the subassembly.)

Many AE2 blocks can be rotated, just because the guide doesn't mention it doesn't mean they can't be rotated.
