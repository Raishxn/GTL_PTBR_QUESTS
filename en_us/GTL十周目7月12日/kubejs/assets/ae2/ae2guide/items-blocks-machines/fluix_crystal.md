#FroixCrystal

<ItemImage id="fluix_crystal" scale="4" />

*"Fruix crystal has the unique ability to absorb and transform energy forms, and is therefore the cornerstone of all material energy technology"*

Fluix crystal is one of the basic synthetic materials for AE2 blocks, [equipment](../ae2-mechanics/devices.md), and items. Made by throwing nether quartz, redstone, and <ItemLink id="charged_certus_quartz_crystal" /> into water.

It is available with <ItemLink id="formation_plane" /> and <ItemLink id="annihilation_plane" />[automation](../example-setups/throw-in-water-automation.md).
