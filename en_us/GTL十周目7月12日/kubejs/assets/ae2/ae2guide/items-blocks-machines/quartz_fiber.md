# quartz fiber

<GameScene zoom="8" background="transparent">
<ImportStructure src="../assets/assemblies/quartz_fiber.snbt" />
<IsometricCamera yaw="195" pitch="30" />
</GameScene>

Quartz fibers transfer energy between [networks](../ae2-mechanics/me-network-connections.md) without connecting them. This also allows you to power [subnetwork](../ae2-mechanics/subnetworks.md) without placing energy receivers and power supply cables everywhere. It also blocks cable connections, but it's cheaper and more effective to use off-colored cables or <ItemLink id="cable_anchor" />.

Quartz fiber is [cable subparts](../ae2-mechanics/cable-subparts.md).
