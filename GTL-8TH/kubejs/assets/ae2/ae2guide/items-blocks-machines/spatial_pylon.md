# space tower

<BlockImage id="spatial_pylon" p:powered_on="true" scale="8" />

The spatial tower is used in [Spatial IO](../ae2-mechanics/spatial-io.md) to generate spatial fields and determine the area of ​​influence.

Each continuous space tower segment occupies 1 [channel](../ae2-mechanics/channels.md).

Space tower segments need to be at least 2 blocks long to function properly.
