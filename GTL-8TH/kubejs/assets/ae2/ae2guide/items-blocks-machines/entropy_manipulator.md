# Entropy change manipulator

<ItemImage id="entropy_manipulator" scale="4" />

Right-click and Shift-right click with the Entropy Manipulator to heat or cool things respectively. It doesn't have many functions, it can only evaporate or freeze water, solidify lava into obsidian, smelt logs into charcoal, and smelt cobblestone into stone.

If no special action can be performed on the block, it behaves the same as flint and steel.

It can be charged in <ItemLink id="charger" />.
