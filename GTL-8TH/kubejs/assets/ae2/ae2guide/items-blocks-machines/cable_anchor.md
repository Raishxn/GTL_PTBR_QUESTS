Small, decorative spikes attached to cables can prevent them from connecting, turn them into ladders, and make them appear connected to surrounding walls. Also used to synthesize <ItemLink id="facade" />.

Cable anchors prevent connections from forming where they are attached.

It can be climbed like a ladder.
