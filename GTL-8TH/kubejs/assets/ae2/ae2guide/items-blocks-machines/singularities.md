# singularity

<ItemImage id="singularity" scale="4" />

An extremely dense ball of matter.

Obtained from <ItemLink id="condenser" /> in Singularity Mode by aggregating 256,000 items or 256,000 barrels of fluid.

## Quantum entangled state singularity

<ItemImage id="quantum_entangled_singularity" scale="4" />

The necessities for establishing a connection between two [Quantum Bridge](quantum_bridge.md) must be produced in pairs during manufacturing. Just put one of a pair of quantum entangled state singularities in <ItemLink id="quantum_link" /> of the quantum network bridge to be connected.

A quantum entangled state singularity can be synthesized by triggering a reaction between <ItemLink id="minecraft:ender_pearl" /> or <ItemLink id="ender_dust" /> and <ItemLink id="singularity" />. Any explosive force should trigger a reaction.

<RecipeFor id="quantum_entangled_singularity" />

***Almost any explosion will work, even creeper ones. ***

They must be produced in pairs, but only a single <ItemLink id="singularity" /> is consumed.

It is recommended to use an original anvil to name it when crafting it.
