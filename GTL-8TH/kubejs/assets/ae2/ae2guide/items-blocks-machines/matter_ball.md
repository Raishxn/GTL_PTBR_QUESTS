#matterball

<ItemImage id="matter_ball" scale="4" />

A ball composed of a mass of material, which can be used as ammunition for <ItemLink id="matter_cannon" /> or used to produce [paint balls] (paintballs.md).

Obtained from <ItemLink id="condenser" /> in Material Ball mode by aggregating 256 items or 256 barrels of fluid.
