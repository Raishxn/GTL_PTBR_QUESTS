## Processor Imprint Template

Silicon, logic, computing, and engineering imprint templates are used in the manufacturing process of [processors](processors.md). These four imprint templates are obtained by destroying <ItemLink id="mysterious_cube" /> in [Meteorite](../ae2-mechanics/meteorites.md). They can also be copied in <ItemLink id="inscriber" />.

## Name Imprint Template

Name stamp templates can be used on stampers to name items, similar to anvils,

Right-click with <ItemLink id="certus_quartz_cutting_knife" /> or <ItemLink id="nether_quartz_cutting_knife" /> and put in a metal ingot. Enter the name you want to print and take out the product. You can use one or two name stamping templates in the stamper. If you use two, the combination of the two names will be stamped, with the top slot first and then the bottom slot.
